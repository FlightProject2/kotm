# 00 – Vision & Pillars

## One-liner

**King of the Mountain** is a PC battle royale that plays like *H1Z1: King of the Kill* did in 2016:
fast, loud, arcadey gunfights in third person, everyone parachuting in from their own random point,
cars sliding around dirt roads, helmets popping off heads, and one King left when the gas finishes.

## Reference era (what "2016 edition" means)

We are cloning the game as it existed from the **February 2016 split** of H1Z1 into *King of the Kill*
up to roughly the **end of 2017**, i.e. **before the February 2018 "Combat Update"** that changed
weapon handling, recoil, ADS, and helmet behaviour. Concretely that means:

| Aspect | 2016 behaviour we replicate |
|---|---|
| Camera | Third person by default, over-the-shoulder aim, first-person toggle, scope view for hunting rifle |
| Bullets | True projectiles with visible tracer trails and gravity drop, tuned per gun |
| Headshots | Helmet absorbs one headshot and is destroyed ("helmet pop"), second headshot kills |
| Body armor | Laminated armor soaks torso damage until it breaks |
| Spawn | Every player parachutes in from a **random point in the sky** (no bus, no plane, no map pick) |
| Movement | Unlimited sprint, jump-shooting is legal, crouch spam, no vaulting animation |
| Vehicles | Off-Roader, Police Car, Pickup Truck, ATV. Drifty, loud, fuel-limited |
| Gas | "Revealing safe zone in N" → shrinking circle of toxic gas, damage ramps by phase |
| Loot | Weight-limited inventory, backpacks add capacity, crafting from scrap (bandages, arrows, makeshift armor) |
| Teams | Solo, Duos, Fives. No down-but-not-out state in 2016; death is death |
| Zombies | **None.** KotK removed PvE. |
| Meta | Kills, Top 10s, Skulls, XP, leaderboards, dailies, missions, cosmetic skins by slot |

## Pillars

1. **Skill is aim + movement + positioning, not gear roulette.** Every gun is viable; the AR-15 is the
   backbone. Bullet drop and travel time reward leading targets. Helmets and armor buy one mistake,
   not five.
2. **Every hit means something.** You see a tracer, you hear a distinct crack for helmet / armor / flesh,
   a helmet visibly flies off, blood puffs on flesh hits. There is never ambiguity about whether you hit
   or what you hit.
3. **Random parachute spawn makes the whole map matter.** No hot-drop meta at one town. You land
   alone, gear up, and the map funnels you together via the gas.
4. **Arcade physics, readable combat.** Cars slide, players jump-shot, shotguns two-tap. It is fun before
   it is realistic.
5. **Fast matches.** 25–30 minutes from lobby to King. Queue again in under 30 seconds.
6. **Cosmetics only.** Skins never change stats. No pay-to-win.

## Non-goals

- Not a survival game: no hunger, thirst, base building, zombies.
- Not a realistic milsim: no weapon attachments beyond scope tiers, no stance-based sway model.
- Not a hero shooter: no abilities, no classes, no loadouts. Everyone starts with fists and a parachute.
- No 2018-era features at launch: no Auto Royale, no revive/knock, no ADS slow-walk penalty, no
  arcade modes. (They can be considered post-launch.)

## Target platform & audience

- PC (Windows) via Steam. 60 FPS target on a GTX 1060 / RX 580 class machine at 1080p medium.
- 150 players per match. Solo / Duos / Fives queues.
- Regions: NA East, NA West, EU, (later) SA, AP.
- Audience: players nostalgic for early KotK and BR players who want a faster, less loot-heavy game.

## Name and theme

"King of the Mountain" (KOTM). The map's central landmark is **the Mountain**, a large peak with a
radio tower and a dam on one flank. The final gas circle is random (not biased to the mountain), but the
Mountain is the map's signature silhouette and the winner screen crowns the player "King of the
Mountain". The staging lobby (screenshot 2) sits at an airfield at the Mountain's foot.
