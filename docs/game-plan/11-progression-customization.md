# 11 – Progression & Customization

Everything here is **cosmetic or bragging rights**. Nothing in this document changes in-match stats.

## Currencies (top bar in screenshot 5)

| Currency | Earned by | Spent on |
|---|---|---|
| **XP / Level** | Kills, placement, damage, survival time, dailies | Nothing directly; unlocks level rewards (skins) |
| **Skulls** | Top 10 finishes (10 → 1 skull … 1st → 10 skulls) and kills in Top 10 | Marketplace cosmetics |
| **Fragments** | Missions and dailies | 20 fragments = 1 crate; 5 crates = 1 rare crate |
| **Crowns** | Premium currency (real money) | Cosmetics and crates only |

## XP table (per match)

| Event | XP |
|---|---|
| Kill | 100 (matches the `100 XP` popup) |
| Headshot kill | +25 |
| Assist | 30 |
| Damage dealt | 1 per 4 dmg |
| Survive to Top 50 / 25 / 10 / 5 | 100 / 200 / 400 / 600 |
| Win | 1000 |
| First win of the day | +500 |
| Minutes survived | 5 / min |

Levels: 1–100, XP curve roughly `500 × level^1.4`. Level rewards every 5 levels (nameplate, skin).

## Missions (menu centre)

Tiered mission tracks: **Warmup → Easy → Medium → Hard → Expert**. Each tier is a set of 5
account-wide goals (e.g. "Get 20 kills with the AR-15", "Finish Top 10 three times in Fives").
Completing a tier awards Fragments and a tier skin. Progress bars under each tier icon.

## Dailies

Four daily tasks reset at 00:00 UTC (screenshot 5 list): e.g. `Kill 1 opponent in Fives 0/1`,
`Complete 3 Fives matches 0/3`, `Kill 13 opponents in Duos 0/13`, `Kill 5 opponents with any
pistol in the top 10 0/5`. Each awards XP + Fragments. One free reroll per day.

## Leaderboards

- Per season, per mode (Solo / Duos / Fives), per region and global.
- Score = placement points + kill points, top 50 matches count **(tune)**. 2017 introduced tiered
  ranks (Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Diamond → Master → Royalty); we ship the tier ladder
  from the start as it is the best-loved part of the meta.
- Tabs: Score, Kills, Wins, Top 10s, K/D. Friends filter.

## Seasons

8-week seasons. Each season: new leaderboard, new mission tier skins, one new crate. Rank tier
badges displayed on nameplates in the lobby.

## Customization (screenshot 3)

Slots: **Head/Hat, Face, Chest, Legs, Feet, Hands, Back, Parachute, Weapon skins** (per weapon),
**Vehicle skins** (per vehicle).

Rarity tiers: Common (white) → Uncommon (green) → Rare (blue) → Ultra-Rare (purple) → Legendary
(gold). Rarity is purely visual prominence; a Common hat and a Legendary hat protect identically
(zero, hats are cosmetic; helmets are separate functional loot).

Launch skin catalogue target: ~300 items across slots, 40 weapon skins, 12 vehicle skins,
8 parachutes. Style is the original's: street/urban, camo, animal prints, ridiculous hats, police
and military pieces.

Skins persist onto the in-match character; found helmets and armor render **over** the cosmetic
head/chest items so the functional state is always readable at a glance.

## Marketplace

- Direct purchase of individual skins with Skulls (earned) or Crowns (bought).
- **Crates** open to a random skin from a listed pool with visible odds. Crates are obtainable via
  Fragments (earned) so every crate is reachable without paying. Duplicates convert to Skulls.
- Player-to-player trading and a Steam-market style economy: **not at launch** (regulatory and
  anti-fraud complexity). Consider later.
- Regional pricing and no gambling-adjacent mechanics beyond the odds-disclosed crate. Where local
  law restricts loot boxes, crates are replaced by direct fragment redemption.

## Account & social

- Friends list (Steam friends imported), party of up to 5, invite/ready-up flow, recent players
  list (for reporting and adding), block/mute persistent.
- Report from the death screen and recent players: cheating, team-killing, voice abuse.
