# KOTM reference: 2016 H1Z1 King of the Kill

Research date: 8 September 2026. Target: the late-2016 PC Battle Royale experience after the September UI update, before the 2017 combat overhaul. This is a functional reference for KOTM's own world, artwork and player models. It is not a claim of frame-exact H1Z1 emulation.

## Gameplay specification and implementation audit

| System | Desired behaviour | KOTM status / verification |
|---|---|---|
| Character | Actual KOTM body, fitted clothing, weapons and shared rig in matches and appearance preview | Integrated GLB, 54 bones and 42 clips; source-presence and build-bundle checks prevent the old mannequin silently returning. |
| AR handling | One accepted click per shot; timely input, accurate paced taps, predominantly lateral kick; dispersion grows under rapid fire and recovers when pausing | Implemented with buffered semi-auto input, per-shot bloom and alternating lateral feedback. Values are KOTM tuning; exact historical cadence/spread curves remain unverified. |
| Projectiles | Finite travel, lead and drop; no firing through cover because a muzzle clipped through it | Substepped rays, per-weapon gravity, camera/muzzle convergence, eye-to-muzzle cover check and authoritative tracer impact stops. Character collision follows its transform even when animation LOD skips poses. |
| Hit registration | Visible head/torso/limbs remain hittable while moving, aiming, crouching and seated | Bone-driven regions; head centre and pelvis coverage corrected. Thin near-miss assistance is an explicit KOTM tolerance, not a measured 2016 rule. |
| Helmet | A headshot destroys protection; attacker sees a brief pop; destroyed helmet cannot be picked up or survive in the death bag | Ordinary helmet hits now use body damage; hunting rifle remains lethal. Pop follows the posed head and fades, with no reusable loot. Same-blast shotgun pellets share helmet protection. |
| Running | Feet and upper body share phase; firing/reload must not restart leg motion | Shared phase and actual-speed gait selection. Empty hands now use an opposing arm swing with bent elbows and relaxed curled fingers, while weapon poses retain their grip. Parachuting uses fixed risers and palm contact; the truck grip accounts for the wrist-to-palm offset and wheel orientation. Exported clips remain editable; further artistic comparison needs accessible dated footage. |
| Stamina | Slower sprint, exhaustion, recovery; looted sneakers prevent exhaustion | KOTM-specific requested rule: walk 2.8 m/s, sprint 5.2 m/s, exhausted 2.4 m/s; eight seconds of reserve, 0.8 s recovery delay, resume at 35%. Cosmetic footwear alone grants no benefit. |
| Vehicles | Distinct acceleration/top speed, braking, rolling after exit; visible/hittable driver through windows | Removed acceleration multiplier and exit speed reset; low rolling resistance. Separate opaque bullet hull leaves glass open. Truck pelvis raised while wrists/ankles retain control contacts. |
| HUD | Remaining count, compass, kill feed, gas warning/timer, reticle/confirmed hit, ammo, health/bleeding, gear, hotbar, contextual loot prompt and map | Combat HUD extended with stamina, bloom feedback, reload/medical countdown and MM:SS zone timer. Tab opens vicinity/carried/crafting columns. Exact 2016 spacing and reticle options still need visual comparison. |
| BR loop | Parachute, loot, shrinking hazard, kill/death, last survivor and match results | Existing KOTM loop; the alpine map and whiteout are intentional KOTM adaptations. Do not silently replace its summit rules or terrain with Z2. |
| Loot/gear | Collect weapons/ammo/medical/armour/backpacks; visible outfit follows protective gear; loot dead players | Actual sneaker pickup changes stamina and visible shoes. Inventory shows carried items and takes whole nearby entries. Materials now survive in death bags. Stack splitting, selective bag looting and garment shredding remain to implement. |
| Medical/crafting | Bleeding management, interrupted timed healing, makeshift armour and coagulant crafting | Tested atomic recipes consume real inventory/equipped ingredients. Coagulant stops bleeding after one second; medical use cancels reload. The two-cloth bandage recipe is KOTM tuning. Five-stage bleeding and historical one/two-hit body-armour durability remain unimplemented; current armour uses a durability pool across non-head regions. |
| Multiplayer | Authoritative damage, remote confirmations, replication and fair hit processing under latency | Existing authority/event infrastructure is a starting point. A browser bot match is not a 150-human load test; lag compensation, matchmaking and squad play require separate end-to-end validation. |
| Meta systems | Appearance/settings/training; optional progression | Preserve KOTM's current appearance, crates and progression. Daybreak accounts, Twitch integration and paid currencies are service-specific, not required to reproduce shooting or the BR loop. |

## Tuning and acceptance

AR velocity remains 620 m/s; gravity is now 2.1 × 9.81 m/s². Ideal horizontal drop is approximately 0.268 m at 100 m and 1.072 m at 200 m. These are calculated KOTM values, not retrieved H1Z1 measurements. The 600-RPM cap only limits input acceptance; paced firing is more accurate than clicking at the cap. At a constant level aim, the projectile launches level and subsequently falls; camera feedback never permanently changes stored mouse aim.

Test a timed ten-shot group, a rapid ten-shot group, recovery after pausing, moving/crouched/airborne groups, and 25/100/200 m moving targets. Reject shots through walls and lower vehicle panels. Verify window headshots, one-hit helmet removal, and a subsequent unprotected hit. Check no reusable helmet loot is created. Test stamina both with bare feet and after an actual sneaker pickup; walk and then sprint after recovery. Exit a moving and a stopped vehicle. Check running, aiming and seated contact positions on the actual GLB, not a proxy capsule.

Local verification: 100 unit/scene tests passed, followed by targeted cover and inventory-menu regression tests. Godot rendered the actual character in four running phases, the truck seat and the inventory. A side-window physics ray reaches the driver's posed head; a lower-door ray is blocked. Wrist and ankle contact tests retain the truck controls. The Python damage table matches the GDScript expectations. CI repeats the complete suite and exports the actual model bundle before publication.

Remaining reference work: measure dated gameplay for precise movement, camera and weapon curves; implement and validate five-stage bleeding, historical armour hit counts, selective bag management/shredding, vehicle keys/hotwiring/righting and exposure rules; then validate squads and real network latency. Gas toxicity proposals require shipped-version confirmation before replacing KOTM's whiteout rules. Current extra weapons, including Hellfire, are KOTM additions rather than a pure 2016 arsenal.

“Exactly the same as 2016” requires measured footage/build evidence and playtesting beyond parameter names. This release implements the core changes above; it does not claim every 2016 system or 150-player service parity.
